# pagination: Figma frame vs live build

Verdict: **major**. Blockers 0, major 5, minor 2.

| Area | Figma frame | Built | Fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cell geometry | Wide, short pills: 32x18 px at size sm on the stage (digit centred with ~10 px side padding), 24x16 px in the states strip; every slot, including the ellipsis and the ghost arrows, keeps the same 32 px width | Square cells (`aspect-square` in CELL_SIZE): 40x40 at md, 24x24 at the smallest size; the 10 px chevron and 10 px digit sit in a box 2.2x the frame's height | Drop `aspect-square`; give a cell `min-w` = its control height plus `px-pad-x-*` so it reads ~1.8:1 wide. Height cannot go below 24 px (R-5.6-2), so match the frame's width and proportion, not its 18 px | major |
| radius | Fully rounded pill on every cell (the curve spans the whole 18 px height; Try-it shows `shape: rounded`) | `rounded-control` = 8 px corners on a 40 px square; reads as a rounded square, not a pill (the 9999px radius exists only as the ocean theme knob) | Add the frame's `shape` prop (`rounded` -> `rounded-full`, `square` -> `rounded-control`) or make `rounded-full` the pagination cell radius | major |
| border of unselected cells | 1 px hairline at (38,38,38) on the (10,10,10) stage = neutral-800 = `--tc-line`; the cells are almost borderless | 1 px at (161,161,161) = neutral-400 = `--tc-line-strong` (BORDER_STRONG in CELL_OTHER); every cell reads as a bright outlined box | Use `BORDER` (border-line) instead of `BORDER_STRONG` in CELL_OTHER; if a 3:1 control boundary is required by rule, record the deviation from the frame in DECISIONS.md | major |
| disabled arrows (first/prev on page 1, next/last on the last page) | Ghost: no border, no box, a muted chevron (~82-106 grey) centred in the same 32 px slot | The full bordered 40x40 box kept at `opacity-50` (border 86, chevron 114 grey) | On `data-disabled` drop the border and the hover tint (`data-disabled:border-transparent`), keep the slot width, colour the chevron `text-muted` instead of fading the whole box | major |
| disabled state | A `disabled` state: every cell ghost, no borders, no primary fill on the current page, digits (114,114,114) muted, chevrons (69-80) muted | No disabled prop or sample; the closest, the single-page sample, keeps the primary fill on page 1 and the bordered boxes on all four arrows | Add a `disabled` boolean (R-5.7-1): render every cell as a `data-disabled` span, drop the current fill and the borders, muted text and icons | major |
| gap between cells | 6 px at the 18 px stage cell, 3 px at the 16 px strip cell (about a third of the height) | `gap-1` = 4 px at the 40 px cell (a tenth of the height); at sm the gap is 4 vs the frame's 6 | Scale the gap with size in CELL_SIZE or LIST (gap-1.5 at sm, gap-2 at md) | minor |
| ellipsis | Three centred middle dots (10 px wide) in muted grey (160-205), vertically centred in the cell | "..." three periods in body colour (250) sitting on the baseline, in the lower third of the 40 px cell | Render U+22EF or three middle dots and use `text-muted` instead of `text-body` in CELL_GAP | minor |

Missing states: disabled

Notes: The "basic" capture (preview-pagination-basic.png) is a 404 page ("This page does not exist"), so the stage sample was checked against the states capture plus the playground and custom-size captures from the same run, which render the same build. Sample content differs and is not counted: the frame's stage is page 1 of 24 (1 2 3 ··· 24) while the build shows 12 pages; the frame's strip uses pageCount 2 (and its "middle" sample shows page 1 selected, its "last page" sample shows no selected page, both authoring slips) while the build uses 12. What matches: the selected cell is primary solid (21,93,252) with a white digit in both; enabled chevrons and digits are body-coloured and the same size (10-11 px glyphs, text-sm) in both; the cell interior is transparent in both; slot widths stay constant across arrows, digits and the ellipsis in both. The frame's 18 px (stage) and 16 px (strip) cell heights are below the 24 px target floor (R-5.6-2), so the build cannot match the height itself; the actionable differences are the proportion, the pill radius, the hairline border and the ghost treatment of disabled controls. Hover and focus are not drawn in the frame. Font family was ignored as instructed.
