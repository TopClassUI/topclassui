Notes: Sample content differs and is not counted: the frame shows value 12 while the basic capture shows 2 with a demo-supplied "Guests" label; the live states demo renders empty (placeholder "0" in muted #a1a1a1), invalid, disabled and read-only (neutral-soft #202020 surface, steppers at 50%), two of which the frame does not draw. Matches: 40px height, ~8px radius, surface neutral-800 #262626, body-white 14px tabular value, 50% opacity for disabled, 11px thin-stroke icons, danger red-600 for invalid. The three missing states are interaction states a static capture cannot show; the frame draws hover and focus-visible identically as a 1px #a1a1a1 border on the box with no offset ring or colour change, whereas the build uses FOCUS_RING_WITHIN (an offset `--tc-ring` primary ring) and HOVER.ghost on the step buttons rather than the box, so expect a visible difference there too. The frame's at-limit state draws the red 1px border with value 99 and the plus at roughly 40% opacity; the build dims the limited step via DISABLED but has no red border at a limit. Verify those three in a browser before closing the parity check. Font-family differences were ignored as instructed.
