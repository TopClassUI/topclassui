Notes: Sample content differs and is not counted: the frame's stage shows 'Ship it everywhere' with a two-line body and two footer buttons; the live basic demo shows 'Storage' with header and body only (no footer), and the footer was compared through the 'With actions' state ('Version 0.4'). The frame's Try it panel exposes variant, padding, media and interactive props that CardRootProps does not have; only the visual consequences (hover, focus-visible) are counted. The build adds a Loading state the frame does not draw: a plain 5:2 block at #161616 (bg-neutral-soft) with no title/body placeholder bars, darker than the card it stands in for; the frame's state thumbnails draw a card as two #202020 bars (8px and 6px tall) and could inform a skeleton with bars. The focus ring in the frame's strip is sized to the whole state cell (it encloses the label), a Figma layout quirk, not a design intent.
