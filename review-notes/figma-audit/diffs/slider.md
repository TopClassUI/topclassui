Missing states: hover, active, focus-visible

Notes: Matches: 6px track height (SLIDER_TRACK_SIZE md h-1.5), #202020 neutral-soft track, #155DFC primary fill, #262626 surface-raised knob with a 2px primary ring, pill radius, thumb centred on the track, and the disabled state at 50% opacity on every part (frame #163A89 / #1E1E1E, build #0F3383 / #181818, both exactly half). Sample content differs and is not counted: the build's basic demo wraps the slider in a Field so a "Volume" label sits above it, while the frame's stage draws two bare sliders (382px and 460px) with no label or hint even though its code tab shows label="Volume" hint="Drag, or use the arrow keys"; the build's states demo also includes a two-thumb invalid range that the frame never draws. The live states capture has four rows (idle, invalid, disabled, invalid range), so hover, active and focus-visible could not be compared; in the frame hover and active are pixel-identical to idle (same 18px thumb, same ring) and focus-visible is a 2px #51A2FF ring at a 2px offset (26px overall), which the build's `outline-ring` at --tc-ring-width/--tc-ring-offset should reproduce (28px around its 20px knob) but needs a captured focus run to confirm. Frame authoring issues to fix in Figma, not in code: in the states strip each thumb is drawn under the next cell's track (its centre reads #202020), the focus ring is laid out as a sibling 23px to the right of its thumb instead of around it, the active thumb sits 22px past the end of its fill (a 44px target wrapper in auto-layout), and the invalid thumb is clipped by the frame's right edge. Font-family differences ignored as instructed.
