# switch: Figma frame vs live build

Verdict: **major**. Blockers 0, major 4, minor 1.

| Area | Figma frame | Built | Fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| geometry / track size (md) | Track is 42 x 24 px in the stage and in every strip state (pill, fully rounded). | Track is 35 x 20 px in both the basic and the states demo (7 px narrower, 4 px shorter). | Make the md control track 42 x 24 (height from the control-size knob, width = 2 x thumb + 2 px) and rescale the other sizes from it so md matches the frame. | major |
| geometry / thumb size | Thumb is a 20 px circle with a 2 px inset from the track edge (on state: 20 px of primary, 20 px of white, 2 px of primary). | Thumb is a 16 px circle with a 2 px inset. | Thumb = track height - 4 px (20 px at md); travel distance = track width - thumb - 4 px. | major |
| surface / off thumb colour | Off thumb is #262626 (raised-surface role) on a #202020 track: nearly invisible, 6 levels of difference. | Off thumb is #A1A1A1 (bright muted grey) on a #202020 track, clearly visible. | Confirm the intended token with the frame owner: if the frame is right, the off thumb takes the raised-surface role (bg-surface-raised); if the frame mis-maps the dark token, fix the frame instead of the build. The on-state thumb (#FFFFFF) already matches. | major |
| state / invalid | Invalid = 2 px #E7000B ring at a 1 px offset outside the track, plus the track's own border tinted dark red (~#7F0B11, 2 px incl. anti-alias); track fill and thumb unchanged. | Invalid = the track border itself becomes 2 px solid #D3020C; no offset ring, no gap. | Use the danger ring from shared/classes.ts (2 px, 1 px offset, --tc-ring width) and set the 1 px border to the danger line at half strength; do not thicken the border. | major |
| line / off track border | 1 px border #A1A1A1 around the off track (reads as line-strong). | 1 px border #939393 around the off track. | Use the same line-strong token the frame uses for the off border (#A1A1A1 in dark). | minor |

Missing states: focus-visible (frame draws a 2 px #51A2FF ring at a 1 px offset around the unchanged off track; not captured in the states demo), hover (frame draws it identical to off: no colour, border or thumb change; not captured in the states demo)

Notes: Sample content differs and is not counted: the basic demo renders two labelled switches ("Weekly digest" on, "Email me when I am mentioned" off, label 10 px to the end side), while the Figma stage shows two bare switches (off, on). Colours that match: on track #155DFC, on thumb #FFFFFF, off track #202020, disabled = whole control at ~50% opacity in both (Figma border #3A3A3A / track #1B1B1B / thumb #1E1E1E vs build #4E4E4E / #151515 / #565656, same ratio on their own base colours). The build's states demo shows 7 samples (off, on, invalid-off, invalid-on, disabled-off, disabled-on, on again); invalid-on and disabled-on are not drawn in the frame, so they were not compared. No shadow on the thumb in either. The frame's off thumb being near-invisible (#262626 on #202020) looks like a dark-mode token mapping on the Figma side rather than a deliberate design, so the "off thumb colour" row should be confirmed before changing the build. Font-family differences ignored as instructed.
