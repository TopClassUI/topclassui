Missing states: hover, focus-visible

Notes: Sample content differs and is not counted: the frame's stage is Light/Dark/Ocean with 16 px icons (6 px gap) while the basic demo is Board/Table/Calendar without icons; the states strip is A/B at a smaller ~32 px track while the live states demo is at md; the live states demo adds invalid (2 px danger ring), read-only and one-disabled samples that the frame does not draw. hover and focus-visible are listed as missing because the server-rendered states capture cannot show them; the frame's hover sample itself draws no tint or pill (identical to an unselected idle), and the build has no hover class either, so the two agree on 'no hover affordance'. Measurements were taken by pixel-scanning the PNGs (scripts in the session scratchpad), not by eye.
