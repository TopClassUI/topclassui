**Check.** Negative test: check-structure predicates fail on broken inputs at scripts/lib/overlay-parts.test.ts:35-51 (Body removed, Header/Footer without Body) and :63-80 (sticky constant without the variable, mention-only). The e2e measures are shown broken samples at overlay-scroll.spec.ts:250-290. The last of those (:282-289) is near-tautological: it sets the padding to 0 and asserts 0, and never runs the positive poll at :233.

Scope: check-structure walks every folder in src/components by readdir (check-structure.ts:174), including parts/ (:140). Dummy and test files are excluded from the sticky scan. The scrolling-overlay set is a hand list, `SCROLLING_OVERLAYS = ['dialog','alert-dialog','drawer']` (overlay-parts.ts:4), plus a Header/Footer heuristic gated on `category === 'overlays'` (:41). That gate means a Select, Combobox or DatePicker that grew a Header/Footer would not be caught, because their category is not overlays. The e2e scope is also a hand list: WITH_BODY :78-81, POPUPS :122-128, plus Command and Toast.

Misses:
- `unpublishedStickyEdges` accepts any quoted occurrence of the variable name (test :69 passes a constant that is never written), so it proves a mention, not a `setProperty` write.
- It sees only `STICKY_TOP`/`STICKY_BOTTOM`. A bar written with raw `sticky top-0` or `fixed bottom-0` passes; Toast is exactly that (toast/classes.ts:11).
- `STICKY_BOTTOM` does not exist in src/shared/classes.ts (only STICKY_TOP at :146), so the bottom half checks nothing.
- PLAN 5.2's `STICKY_EDGE` (PLAN.md:449) is named STICKY_TOP in code.
- Nothing proves that the class-level bounds come from a shared constant (46.1).
- Nothing checks that the active item is scrolled into view with `block:'nearest'`, or that a submenu never scrolls its parent (PLAN.md:1563).
- Nothing checks the Drawer's safe-area padding or `Drawer.VirtualKeyboardProvider` (PLAN.md:1566).
- The overscroll measure only looks inside `[data-base-ui-portal]` (spec :16), so an overlay rendered without a portal escapes it.

Component changes: none. The diff touches only docs, scripts and e2e. RULES.md status lines are rewritten for R-9.2-4, R-10.5-1 and R-10.5-3. The DECISIONS entry (DECISIONS.md:9-20) holds four of the departures above. The spec's 4-line header comment (:7-10) is outside R-5.12-3's src/ scope, but it is the kind of paragraph that rule bans.
